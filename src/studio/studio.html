Preview every effect here. It all carries over to Meet, Zoom, Teams, Discord and any site that uses your camera.

You're set up

  1. Turn on your camera below and pick a face or a preset.
  2. Step out of frame for a second so SpookCam can learn the room behind you — that's what makes vanishing look real.
  3. Press Snap (or Alt ⇧ S during any call) and turn to dust. Press again to come back.

Already in a call? Turn your camera off and on once so SpookCam can attach to it.
